Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

PixPress is a photo compression app built around one core promise: your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what that means, what limited app data is collected to keep PixPress running and free, and what is never collected at all.

Photos, signatures and documents

PixPress compresses, resizes and converts photos entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to a PixPress server, there is no server that touches your images at any point. Compressed files are saved to a location you choose on your device, and PixPress has no way to access, copy or transmit them afterward.

Metadata (EXIF and location data)

Photos can carry embedded metadata, including GPS location, camera model and timestamp. PixPress reads this metadata locally so you can choose to strip GPS location or all EXIF data before you save or share a photo. This metadata is never read, collected or transmitted by PixPress outside your device, it is only ever used to give you control over what stays in the file you export.

Compression history

PixPress keeps a local history of your past compressions (thumbnails, file sizes and settings used) so you can revisit or re-export a result later. This history is stored only on your device, in the app's own local storage, and is not synced to any server. You can clear it at any time from within the app.

Permissions PixPress uses

PixPress requests only the permissions it needs to do its job, and nothing is sent off-device as a result of using them:

  • Photos / media access, to let you pick photos to compress and save the results back to your device.
  • Camera, only if you choose to capture a new photo directly from within the app.
  • Notifications, some fire locally on your device, such as a compression-complete alert, and don't involve a server at all. Others, like occasional reminders or announcements, are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM notifications are broadcast to all installs by topic, not targeted to you individually or based on any personal or usage data.

Data PixPress does not collect

PixPress does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts. Creating an account is never required to use the app.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

PixPress offers optional ad-credit rewards and a Pro subscription for extra features. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, and subscription status is managed through a third-party subscription management service. PixPress does not see or store your payment card details, those are handled entirely by Google Play. The subscription management service receives purchase and subscription status information needed to unlock the features you've paid for; it does not receive your photos.

Third-party services

PixPress uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob, to show ads and, if you choose to, offer rewarded ads in exchange for compression credits.
  • Firebase Analytics, to understand app usage in aggregate (e.g. which features are used).
  • Firebase Crashlytics, to diagnose crashes and improve stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), to deliver occasional broadcast notifications (reminders, announcements) to all installs by topic.
  • Google Play Billing and a third-party subscription management service, to process optional in-app purchases and subscriptions.

These services may collect limited device and app information, such as:

  • device identifiers (e.g. Advertising ID, FCM registration token, depending on your device settings)
  • app interactions (e.g. screens viewed, feature usage)
  • diagnostic information (e.g. crash logs, device model, OS version)
  • purchase and subscription status (for accounts using in-app purchases)

None of these services ever receive your photos, signatures or documents.

Your choices

  • You can manage ad personalization and device advertising settings in your Google/Android settings.
  • You can disable notifications for PixPress at any time in your device's system settings.
  • You can clear PixPress's local compression history from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored PixPress data from your device.

Children's privacy

PixPress is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by PixPress, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PixPress changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how PixPress handles data, contact us at uds.apps.dev@gmail.com.
